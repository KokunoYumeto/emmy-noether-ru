# RU001-EDIT-0011 — Paper 2 five-column schema and rosette restoration

Status: applied, built, visually checked, and independently replayed on 2026-08-14. This is a working-successor decision; it has no publication or DOI effect.

## Decision

Restore the Paper 2 rectangular schema from four columns to the five columns carried by the active German ED0014 source, restore the omitted terminal row of the upper schema, and place the same verified source rosette at the upper continuation point and beside the lower `*)` attachment marker.

The Russian explanatory prose and the localized lower source note remain byte-for-byte unchanged. No terminology, sentence wording, formula variable, citation, label, or cross-reference was edited.

## Why this was necessary

The inherited Russian layout was not merely a stylistic simplification. It omitted a complete fifth column, the terminal continuation row, and both attachment marks that tell the reader how the lower fragment joins the upper schema. That made the displayed construction materially less legible and less faithful to the named source.

The repair follows ED0014 lines 606–658 and the retained printed/GDZ witnesses. The embedded rosette is the already adjudicated 108×108 authority asset; it is used twice with the same fixed size, advance, and vertical placement. The `*)` source-note relationship is explicit rather than inferred from page position.

## Alternatives rejected

- Keeping the four-column display: it preserves a known omission, not a defensible variant.
- Replacing the rosette with a generic star: it loses the source’s two matching attachment marks.
- Redrawing or smoothing the rosette: the accepted source-derived bitmap already has a pinned custody and placement record.
- Rewording the Russian note while touching the display: the note is semantically adequate and was preserved exactly.
- Editing the frozen v018 or public r35 files: this decision belongs only to the isolated Russian successor.
