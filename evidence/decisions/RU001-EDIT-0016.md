# RU001-EDIT-0016 - Paper 14's historical `Primärzahlen`

## Decision

At the two Furtwängler loci, use **сингулярные примарные числа** rather
than **особые первичные числа**. No other Paper 14 use of `первичн-` is
changed.

## Why

The German source says `singuläre Primärzahlen` twice. At the first locus it
also says `Primzahl l` in the same sentence. That local contrast is decisive:
`Primärzahl` is a historical technical predicate, while `Primzahl` is an
ordinary prime number.

The domain-matched Russian evidence supports the technical register. The
Russian State Library catalogue identifies the 1940 Russian translation of
Erich Hecke's *Lectures on the Theory of Algebraic Numbers*, translated by
G. I. Olshanskii and D. A. Raikov. Its publicly indexed table of contents
names sections 61 and 62 **Сингулярные примарные числа**. Independently,
V. M. Gritsenko's 1973 Russian paper on arithmetic codes uses
`примарных чисел` and `примарное число` while discussing higher reciprocity
laws, and defines the property on the following page.

This evidence is stronger and more specific than the inherited paraphrase.
It also keeps three senses visibly separate:

- `первичны ... вторичны` for ordinary primary/secondary relations;
- `первичный идеал` for the edition's already sealed primary-ideal register;
- `сингулярное примарное число` for the historical reciprocity-theory term.

## Exact scope

- `source/base-papers1-43-ru.tex:10063` and local producer line 243:
  `особых первичных чисел` -> `сингулярных примарных чисел`.
- `source/base-papers1-43-ru.tex:10067` and local producer line 247:
  `особые первичные числа` -> `сингулярные примарные числа`.

The relational controls at base lines 9894 and 9900 and the primary-ideal
controls at line 9969 are byte-preserved. The immutable recovered translation
source is byte-preserved; a local producer shadow records the active edition.

## Rejected alternatives

- Keep `особые первичные числа`: loses both historical technical predicates.
- Use `сингулярные первичные числа`: retains the collision with the separate
  `первичн-` register and disagrees with the matched Russian terminology.
- Use `особые примарные числа`: still paraphrases `singulär` without need.
- Replace `примарн-` or `первичн-` globally: forbidden and semantically wrong.

## Verification and limits

The two changes replay exactly in both the assembled base and the new local
producer. Exact reversal restores the RU001-EDIT-0015 base and the immutable
producer origin byte-for-byte. There are now exactly two `примарн-` tokens in
all four active Russian sources, both at these declared historical loci, and
zero unintended ones. The two old terminology holds are therefore closed.

The RSL catalogue is the bibliographic authority for the 1940 translation;
the exact section heading was checked through a public indexed transcription
because the RSL scan viewer was not accessible in this session. The Gritsenko
article was visually inspected on printed pages 20 and 21. Neither source is
being redistributed in the public package without a separate rights decision,
and this edit does not claim native-speaker certification.

Because the assembled source changed, the existing RU001-EDIT-0015 reader is
now a predecessor artifact. A fresh deterministic build and affected-page
visual check are required before publication.
