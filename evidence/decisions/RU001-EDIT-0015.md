## Defect and evidence

The proof is in Section 8, item 2; its reference to the lemma in Section 7, item 3 is not the location of the defect.

The accepted repair therefore removes only the two target `\not` controls. It preserves the neighboring negative statements for `m`, `m^2`, and `c` modulo the square of the prime ideal, as well as the positive `c` relation and `bn=mc`.
